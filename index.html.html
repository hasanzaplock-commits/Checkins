How it works

Two parts. Zero complexity.

The Portal
Hosted free on GitHub Pages. Team scans the QR code or opens the URL, picks their name, clicks Check In or Check Out. That's the entire workflow — forever.
The Database
A Google Sheet lives silently in the background. Every check-in and checkout is automatically saved. 30 days of history. Nobody needs to touch it.
Fully Automatic
Late flags auto-trigger. Absent marked at 11 PM every weekday. Weekends blocked. Live data synced everywhere. Zero admin work needed from you.
